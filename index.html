About me

Hi, I'm Josh.

Dogs deserve more than a kennel when their people travel. I offer attentive, one-at-a-time (or small-group) care so each dog feels known — not just looked after.

Expect regular walks, playtime, meals on their schedule, and updates while you're away. If your dog has medication, a favourite toy, or a particular bedtime ritual, I'll follow it.

  • Calm, home-style environment
  • Daily photo or message updates
  • Happy to follow feeding and meds notes
